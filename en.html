About the Doctor

Dr. Ahmed AlMohammed

Consultant Dermatologist & Aesthetic Medicine

Saudi Board 2020 Arab Board 2020 Dermatology Consultant

Dr. Ahmed AlMohammed is a Consultant in Dermatology and Cosmetology, holding the Saudi Board in Dermatology and the Arab Board in Dermatology (2020), and is classified as a Dermatology Consultant by the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties.

He has specialized experience in treating various skin diseases and non-surgical cosmetic medicine, including Botox, dermal fillers, collagen stimulators, PRP, skin boosters, Morpheus8, fractional laser, and skin rejuvenation.

He also provides diagnosis and treatment for acne, eczema, psoriasis, alopecia, hair loss, pigmentation, melasma, and scars using the latest medical technologies.
